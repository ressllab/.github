We work on the structural neurobiology of the synapse and the proteins involved within. Synaptic proteins are often dysregulated in several brain function disruptions across memory, neurodevelopment disorders, and metabolic conditions. Solving the mechanism of how various proteins play a role in synapse organization will allow us to treat these conditions at the molecular level. The Ressl Lab is run by Dr. Susanne Ressl, Ph.D. at the University of Texas at Austin.
